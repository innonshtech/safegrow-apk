Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Effective Date: October 16, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 16, 2026
Applications: SafeGrow Mobile (Android & iOS) • Package ID: com.safegrow.app / com.safegrowmobile

📍 Prominent Disclosure: Background Location Tracking (Google Play & Apple Compliance)

SafeGrow collects precise and approximate location data, including in the background (when the app is closed or not in use), exclusively to enable field sales route tracking, travel distance calculation, and attendance verification during your active work shift.

  • Location tracking is only active after you tap “Check-In” and completely stops the moment you tap “Check-Out”.
  • SafeGrow never tracks your location off-duty, during personal hours, weekends, or approved leaves.
  • Location data is never sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

1. Overview & Scope

SafeGrow (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is an enterprise field workforce management platform provided to corporate employers (“Organizations”) and their authorized sales representatives and field personnel (“Users”). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, processing, and protection in compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, Apple App Store Review Guidelines, and applicable data privacy regulations.

2. Categories of Information Collected

A. Personal & Organization Data

Full Name, Employee ID, assigned sales territory, phone number, email address, reporting manager, and organizational role (ADMIN, MANAGER, REP). Passwords are cryptographically hashed using salted bcrypt.

B. Geolocation Data (Foreground & Background)

GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, speed, horizontal accuracy, timestamp) captured during check-in, check-out, client visits, and periodic background tracking pings during active work shifts to substantiate travel allowances and territory coverage.

C. Camera & Live Verification Photos

Live selfies captured at check-in/out to verify employee physical attendance, and on-site photos of vendor/dealer facilities to document visits. The app does not access or browse your private device photo gallery.

D. Device Information & Anti-Fraud Telemetry

Device model, operating system version, unique device installation ID, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens for shift notifications. Anti-fraud telemetry detects mock location / fake GPS apps to safeguard operational accuracy.

3. How We Use the Data

  • Workforce Operations: Recording verified employee attendance and calculating working hours.
  • Travel Expense Validation: Calculating accurate distance covered during duty hours for reimbursement and fuel allowance calculation.
  • Field Sales Validation: Verifying client and dealer visit records, order statuses, and meeting outcomes.
  • Safety & Compliance: Ensuring employee safety during transit and preventing proxy attendance.
  • Transactional Notifications: Sending shift start reminders, sync alerts, and manager announcements via push notifications.

4. Device Permissions Requested

Permission Platform Operational Purpose
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Android Captures precise GPS coordinates during check-in, check-out, and dealer visits.
ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION Android Calculates duty travel distance and logs sales route while app is closed during shifts.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION Android Displays persistent status indicator while location tracking is active for user transparency.
CAMERA Android / iOS Captures live verification selfies for attendance and on-site photos of dealer meetings.
NSLocationAlwaysAndWhenInUse iOS Enables continuous route and distance tracking during active sales duty hours on iOS.

5. Third-Party Service Providers & Zero Advertising

We share data with third-party sub-processors solely to maintain cloud infrastructure and fulfill core functions under binding confidentiality agreements:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS S3): Encrypted cloud storage for attendance selfies and visit documentation photos.
  • Google Maps Platform: Map rendering, route calculation, and reverse geocoding services.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Push notification delivery.

Zero Advertising Policy: SafeGrow contains no third-party advertising SDKs and does not monetize, sell, or trade any user telemetry or personal data.

6. Account Deletion & Data Retention (Google Play & Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v) Compliant)

Users and client organizations have the right to request deletion of their SafeGrow account and associated data at any time:

  1. Through the Mobile App: Go to Profile → Settings → Account → Request Account Deletion.
  2. Via Direct Email: Send an email to info@safegrow.in with your registered Employee ID and Organization name.
  3. Through your Organization Administrator: Administrators can deactivate and purge employee accounts directly via the SafeGrow Admin Portal.

Upon verified request, your personal login credentials, selfie photos, and telemetry trails are permanently purged within 30 business days, subject to statutory labor regulations governing employment records.

7. Data Security & Children's Privacy

Security: All network communication is encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.2/1.3 encryption. Passwords and credentials are cryptographically protected. Media files are secured using presigned, expiring access URLs.

Children's Privacy: SafeGrow is exclusively a business enterprise tool intended for authorized adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in relevant jurisdictions).
